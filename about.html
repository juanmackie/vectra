Supported pattern types

Each curve family has its own beauty and mathematical character. VECTRA includes the following 8 types.

Lissajous Lissajous

Two orthogonal sinusoidal oscillations combined. Change the frequency ratio and phase to produce an infinite variety of elegant knot-like lattice patterns.

Rhodonea Rose curve

The polar equation r = cos(k*theta) creates petal-shaped curves. A single coefficient changes the petal count, revealing floral symmetry.

Spirograph Hypotrochoid

The trace of a pen attached to a smaller circle rolling inside a larger one. Dense, delicate, lace-like patterns from interlocking gears.

Phyllotaxis Phyllotaxis

Points arranged along the golden angle, mimicking sunflower seed spirals. Nature's optimal packing, recreated algorithmically.

Superformula Superformula

Gielis' superformula describes a vast range of organic shapes in a single equation. Stars, flowers, polygons, all from one formula.

Harmonograph Harmonograph

The damped trace of coupled pendulums. Gradually decaying oscillations produce poetic spiral forms that slowly close in.

Clifford Attractor Clifford

A chaotic point cloud from simple iterative equations. Countless points form cloud- and feather-like density gradients.

de Jong Attractor de Jong

Another strange attractor family. Butterfly-like or misty organic forms emerge unpredictably from computation.

Frequently asked questions

What is VECTRA?

A free browser-based tool that generates beautiful geometric patterns from mathematical formulas. Adjust parameters, choose colors, and create unique designs with no math or installation required.

Is it free? Do I need to sign up?

Completely free. No registration or installation needed. Open the page and start creating immediately.

What patterns can I create?

Lissajous curves, Rhodonea (rose) curves, Spirograph (hypotrochoid), Phyllotaxis, Superformula, Harmonograph, Clifford attractor, and de Jong attractor. 8 types in total.

Can I export my patterns?

Yes. Export as SVG (vector) for use in Illustrator or as high-resolution PNG for web, social media, and print.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The interface is optimized for mobile and tablet browsers. On mobile, long-press exported images to save them.
